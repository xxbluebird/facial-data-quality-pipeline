# Facial Image Data Collection Protocol

## Objective

To collect facial images under controlled variations in viewing angle
and lighting conditions for development and evaluation of an automated
image quality-validation pipeline.

## Participant Preparation

Before image acquisition:

1. Explain the purpose of the data collection.
2. Obtain informed consent.
3. Assign a pseudonymous subject ID.
4. Prepare a consistent image-capture environment.
5. Confirm that the participant is ready for image acquisition.

## Camera Setup

Initial proof-of-concept acquisition will use a smartphone camera.

The camera should be positioned at approximately eye level.

A similar distance between the camera and participant should be
maintained across standard captures.

## Viewing Angles

Images will be collected under the following approximate orientations:

- Frontal
- Left approximately 30 degrees
- Right approximately 30 degrees
- Slightly upward
- Slightly downward

## Lighting Conditions

The following lighting conditions may be evaluated:

- Normal indoor lighting
- Front lighting
- Side lighting
- Low lighting
- Backlighting

## Image Quality Assessment

Each image will later be evaluated for:

- Image resolution
- Blur or sharpness
- Brightness
- Face presence
- Number of detected faces

## Data Organization

Each participant receives a pseudonymous identifier such as:

S001

Image filenames follow a structured naming convention.

Example:

S001_S01_frontal_frontlight_001.jpeg

## Quality-Control Outcome

Each image will eventually receive one of two classifications:

ACCEPT

or

REJECT

Rejected images will include a documented rejection reason.
